Contrast budget by bar lightness

The highest APCA contrast any tint of the bar's own hue can reach, as the bar's perceptual lightness sweeps 0→1. The red band is the dead zone: a bar placed there is unreadable no matter which foreground you pick, so bar lightness — not foreground choice — is the real constraint. v1.2.9 puts the light-mode inactive title bar deep below it.
